- Verified: `expo config --type prebuild` resolves the key; `expo prebuild --platform android --clean` writes `com.google.android.geo.API_KEY` into the generated manifest. `expo config --type public` strips it, as expected.
- README: new "View it on an Android phone" section explaining the key, the silent-failure symptom, and that a dev-client rebuild is required after changing it.
